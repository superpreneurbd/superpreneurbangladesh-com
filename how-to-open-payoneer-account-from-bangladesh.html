Payoneer is the payment backbone for most Bangladeshi freelancers — it connects directly to Fiverr and Upwork and lets you withdraw earnings to your local bank. But the signup and verification process trips up a lot of beginners: rejected documents, name mismatches, and accounts stuck "under review" for weeks.

This guide walks you through the whole process, step by step, with the practical details that actually matter.

Before You Start: What You Need

Gather these before you begin — having everything ready makes the process smooth:

  1. A valid photo ID. Your National ID (NID) or passport. It must be current, not expired.
  2. Your legal name, spelled consistently. This is the single most important detail in the whole process.
  3. A Bangladeshi bank account in your own name. A regular savings or current account works. The account holder name must match your Payoneer name exactly.
  4. An email address you actually check. Use a permanent email — not a temporary one, not one you might lose access to.
  5. A phone number for verification. You will receive SMS codes during signup.

    6. You do not need a website, a business license, or existing clients to open an account. Individual freelancers are welcome.

    Step 1: Register on Payoneer's Website

    Go to Payoneer's official website and start the signup. You will be asked what the account is for — choose the option that matches freelancing or receiving payments from clients/marketplaces.

    Fill in your details carefully:

    • Full name: exactly as it appears on your NID or passport. If your ID says "Md. Kamal Hossain," do not write "Kamal Hossain" — use the full version.
    • Date of birth: must match your ID.
    • Address: your current residential address.
    • Country: Bangladesh.

    Why this matters: Payoneer compares every detail against your ID later. Even small differences — a missing "Md.", a different spelling — can trigger a manual review that delays you by days or weeks.

    Step 2: Verify Your Email and Phone

    Payoneer sends a confirmation link to your email and an SMS code to your phone. Complete both promptly. If the SMS does not arrive, check that you entered the country code correctly (+880 for Bangladesh) and request a resend rather than creating a second account.

    Never create two accounts to "speed things up." Duplicate accounts get flagged, and merging them is a slow, painful process. One person, one account.

    Step 3: Submit Your ID for Verification

    This is where most rejections happen. Upload a clear photo or scan of your NID or passport.

    For a smooth verification:

    • Good lighting, no glare. Take the photo near a window in daylight. Flash glare on the ID's shiny surface is the number one reason for rejection.
    • All four corners visible. Do not crop the edges of the ID.
    • Everything readable. If any text is blurry, retake it.
    • No screenshots of photos. Take a fresh photo of the physical ID.
    • File format: JPG or PNG, reasonable size (a few MB is fine).

    Payoneer may also ask for a selfie holding your ID or a short verification video. This is standard — follow the on-screen instructions.

    If your NID has a smart card version, use it — it photographs more clearly than the older laminated cards.

    Step 4: Answer the Business Questions

    Payoneer will ask how you plan to use the account — your occupation, expected monthly volume, and the source of your payments (freelance marketplaces, direct clients, etc.).

    Answer honestly. "Freelancer receiving payments from Fiverr/Upwork clients" is a perfectly normal answer. There is no minimum income requirement — beginners with zero earnings history are accepted.

    Step 5: Link Your Bangladeshi Bank Account

    Add your local bank account for withdrawals:

    • Account holder name: must match your Payoneer account name exactly.
    • Bank name: select from the list or enter it.
    • Account number: double-check every digit. A wrong digit sends your money into the void.
    • Branch/routing details: provide whatever the form asks for — branch name or routing number as required.

    Test with a small withdrawal first once your account is approved and funded. Confirm the money arrives before routing large payments.

    Step 6: Connect to Fiverr or Upwork (If Applicable)

    If you earn from marketplaces:

    • Fiverr: Go to your Fiverr settings → earnings → add Payoneer as a withdrawal method. Fiverr will guide you through linking.
    • Upwork: Go to Settings → Get Paid → add Payoneer as a payment method.

    Linking is straightforward once your Payoneer account is verified. Our comparison of Payoneer vs Wise explains when Payoneer is the better choice for marketplace earnings.

    How Long Does Approval Take?

    Verification typically takes a few business days. During busy periods it can take longer. You can check your status in the account dashboard.

    If your application is stuck "under review" for more than a week:

    1. Check your email (including spam) for requests for additional documents.
    2. Respond to any such request promptly with clear documents.
    3. Contact Payoneer support through the help center with your customer ID ready.

      4. Common Rejection Reasons (And How to Avoid Them)

      • Blurry ID photo. Retake in daylight, all corners visible.
      • Name mismatch. Your signup name must match your ID exactly.
      • Expired ID. Check the expiry date before uploading.
      • Wrong document type. Upload a government-issued photo ID — not a student card or office ID.
      • Duplicate account. If you signed up before and forgot, recover the old account instead of creating a new one.
      • Someone else's bank account. The bank account must be in your own name.

      After Approval: Your Receiving Accounts

      Once approved, Payoneer gives you receiving accounts — virtual bank details in USD, EUR, GBP, and other currencies. You can share these with direct clients so they can pay you via local bank transfer in their country.

      This is separate from marketplace withdrawals — it is how you get paid by clients you find yourself. Share only the details for the currency the client pays in, and always confirm receipt before considering an invoice paid.

      Security: Protect Your Account

      Your Payoneer account holds real money. Treat it accordingly:

      • Enable two-step verification the day your account is approved.
      • Use a unique, strong password — not one you reuse elsewhere.
      • Never share your login with anyone, including "agents" who offer to help you withdraw.
      • Beware of phishing. Payoneer will never ask for your password by email or phone. If a message looks suspicious, log in directly through the website instead of clicking links.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Is Payoneer free to open from Bangladesh?

      Yes, opening a Payoneer account is free. Fees apply later when you receive, convert, or withdraw money — check Payoneer's current fee schedule before you start so there are no surprises.

      Can students open a Payoneer account?

      Yes, as long as you have a valid NID or passport and are of legal age to enter financial agreements. Students freelancing part-time use Payoneer routinely.

      My NID name and bank account name are spelled differently. What do I do?

      Fix the spelling mismatch before signing up — either update the bank account name or use the exact spelling from your NID consistently. Mismatches are a top cause of withdrawal failures.

      How long does verification take?

      Usually a few business days. It can take longer during busy periods or if documents need manual review. Clear, well-lit ID photos speed things up significantly.

      Can I use my parent's or friend's bank account?

      No. The bank account must be in your own name, matching your Payoneer account. Using someone else's account will cause the withdrawal to fail or the account to be flagged.

      What if my application is rejected?

      Read the rejection reason carefully — it is usually a document issue. Fix it (clearer photo, correct name spelling) and resubmit.

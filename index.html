Who this is for

Built for three kinds of people

Job holders

Bankers, teachers, HR professionals, merchandisers — anyone with a full-time job and two free hours a day. Learn a skill after office hours and build income without quitting.

Students abroad

Bangladeshi students in the UK, Canada, Australia and the US who want legal freelance income alongside their studies. Payments, tax basics and visa-aware guidance.

Homemakers

Anyone building income from home around family life. Flexible skills, realistic timelines, and payment methods that work from a home office.

Three paths

Pick the path that fits your life

1. Freelancing

Sell a skill — digital marketing, design, writing, video — to clients on Fiverr, Upwork or directly. The fastest route to real income if you can invest a few focused hours a week. Our complete freelancing guide walks you through it step by step.

2. Digital products

Create once, sell many times: ebooks, templates, short courses. Slower to start, but it keeps earning while you sleep or sit in the office. Your job knowledge becomes the product.

3. Remote work

Full or part-time remote roles with companies abroad. Bangladeshi professionals are increasingly hired remotely — especially in marketing, design and support roles. The hire talent page explains what employers look for.

Every day, millions of people in Bangladesh type questions into Google. "Best restaurant in Dhanmondi." "How to apply for a passport." "Price of iPhone 15 in Dhaka." The websites at the top get the clicks, the customers, and the sales. SEO — search engine optimization — is the skill of making your website one of those top results. The best part? You can learn it free, practice on your own site, and sell it as a freelance service worldwide. This guide breaks down SEO basics for beginners in Bangladesh in plain, simple language.

What Is SEO, Really?

SEO means improving your website so Google shows it higher in search results when people search for things related to your site.

Think of Google as a giant library with billions of "books" (web pages). SEO is how you make sure Google picks your book when someone asks a question.

There are two big pieces:

  • On-page SEO: What is on your pages — titles, headings, words, and images.
  • Off-page SEO: What other websites say about you — mainly links pointing to your site.

You do not need to be a programmer. You need to understand what Google wants: helpful, clear pages that answer what people search for.

How Google Ranks Pages (The Simple Version)

Google's goal is simple: show the best answer first. It looks at hundreds of signals, but for beginners, these matter most:

  1. Relevance. Does your page answer the exact search? A page about "mango price in Rajshahi" should cover mango prices in Rajshahi — not mangoes in general.
  2. Content quality. Thin pages full of fluff lose to pages that genuinely help the reader.
  3. Backlinks. Each link from another site is a vote of trust. Votes from respected sites count more.
  4. User experience. Is the page fast and mobile-friendly? Most Bangladeshis browse on phones.
  5. Authority. Does your site have a track record of good content on this topic?

    6. Google sends "crawlers" to read pages across the internet, then stores ("indexes") them. When someone searches, Google picks from indexed pages. Your first job: make pages worth indexing.

    Keyword Research With Free Tools

    A keyword is the word or phrase people type into Google. Keyword research means finding the phrases your audience actually searches, then writing pages that answer them.

    Here is a beginner-friendly process using only free tools:

    Step 1: Brainstorm topics. Write down 10–20 questions your audience asks. If you run a bakery in Dhaka: "birthday cake price in Dhaka," "where to buy cupcakes in Dhanmondi," "custom wedding cake Dhaka."

    Step 2: Check search volume with Google Keyword Planner. It is free with a Google Ads account (no spending required). It shows roughly how many people search a phrase each month.

    Step 3: Use Ubersuggest (free version). The free plan gives a few searches per day, showing search volume, ranking difficulty, and ranking competitors.

    Step 4: Target long-tail keywords first. These are longer, specific phrases like "affordable SEO services for restaurants in Dhaka." Fewer searches, far less competition — and searchers are closer to buying.

    Step 5: Look at Google's "People also ask." Type your keyword into Google and check the "People also ask" box — real questions from real people. Answer them in your content.

    Golden rule: one page, one topic, one keyword. This keeps content focused and easy for Google to understand.

    On-Page SEO: The Essentials

    On-page SEO is fully in your control. Get these right on every page:

    Title Tags

    The title tag is the clickable headline in Google's results. Rules:

    • Put your main keyword near the start.
    • Keep it under 60 characters so it does not get cut off.
    • Make it interesting enough to click. "Cheap Flights Dhaka to Bangkok: 2026 Price Guide" beats "Flights Page."

    Headings

    Use headings to structure your page like an outline:

    • One H1 per page (the main title).
    • H2s for main sections.
    • H3s for sub-sections inside them.

    Headings help both readers and Google understand what your page covers. Naturally include keywords in some headings — but write for humans first, always.

    Content Quality

    This is where beginners win or lose. Write content that is:

    • Complete. Answer the question fully. Someone searching "how to start freelancing in Bangladesh" wants steps, not three generic sentences.
    • Original. Never copy other sites. Google punishes copied content, and readers can tell.
    • Clear. Short sentences, short paragraphs, plain language — the style of this article.
    • Useful. Give specifics: steps, tools, numbers, examples. "Do good marketing" helps nobody.

    Aim for depth, not word count. An 800-word page that fully answers a question beats a 2,000-word page of filler.

    URLs, Images, and Internal Links

    • URLs: Keep them short and readable: `yoursite.com/seo-basics`, not `yoursite.com/page?id=482`.
    • Images: Use descriptive filenames ("dhaka-birthday-cake.jpg", not "IMG_2041.jpg") and add alt text. This helps Google understand images and helps visually impaired readers.
    • Internal links: Link your own pages together — for example, link your freelancing guide to your pricing guide. Internal links help Google discover pages and keep readers on your site longer.

    Local SEO for Bangladeshi Businesses

    If you serve customers in a specific area — a restaurant, salon, clinic, or shop — local SEO is your biggest opportunity. Here is how to win it:

    1. Create a Google Business Profile. It is free. Add your name, address, phone, hours, and photos — this is what shows in Google Maps and local searches.
    2. Keep details consistent. Your name, address, and phone number must be identical everywhere: website, Facebook page, directories.
    3. Get reviews. Ask happy customers to leave Google reviews — one of the strongest local ranking signals. Respond to every review.
    4. Use location keywords. Target phrases like "dental clinic in Uttara" or "catering service Chattogram."
    5. Stay active on Facebook. Many Bangladeshis find businesses on Facebook first, then verify on Google. Keep both updated.

      Link Building Basics

      Backlinks are the hardest part of SEO — and the most powerful. Start with honest, beginner-friendly methods:

      • Create link-worthy content. Original research, useful guides, and free tools earn links naturally — the best long-term strategy.
      • Guest posting. Write for other Bangladeshi blogs in your niche with a link back to your site. Many owners welcome good free content.
      • Directories and profiles. List your business in relevant Bangladeshi directories and link your site from social profiles.
      • Build relationships. Connect with bloggers and journalists in your niche. Engage with their content before asking for anything.

      What to avoid: never buy links, join link-exchange schemes, or spam comments with your link. Google detects it all — and the penalty (vanishing from search) is worse than having few links.

      How Long Does SEO Take? (The Honest Answer)

      SEO is not instant. Here is a realistic timeline for a new website:

      • Months 1–3: Google discovers and indexes your pages. You may rank for a few long-tail keywords. Traffic is small.
      • Months 3–6: With regular publishing, rankings climb and long-tail keywords bring steady visitors.
      • Months 6–12: Competitive keywords become reachable. Traffic compounds — old posts keep working while new ones add more.
      • Year 2+: A consistent site becomes a traffic asset that brings customers daily without ad spend.

      The honest truth: SEO rewards patience and consistency. Most beginners quit at month two. Those who publish helpful content weekly for a year win.

      Offering SEO as a Freelance Service

      SEO is one of the most in-demand freelance skills worldwide — and Imtiaz Khan teaches that digital marketing skills are among the fastest ways for Bangladeshis to earn online. Here is how to turn your SEO learning into income:

      Step 1: Practice on your own site. Start a blog about something you know and rank it. Your own rankings are your portfolio — they prove more than any certificate.

      Step 2: Learn the core services. Beginners typically sell keyword research, on-page optimization, Google Business Profile setup, and monthly content writing.

      Step 3: Build a portfolio from zero. Do free or low-cost SEO audits for 2–3 local businesses. Document what you fixed and what improved — case studies with real numbers win clients.

      Step 4: Find clients. International clients hire on Upwork and Fiverr. Locally, approach businesses with a weak Google presence — many Bangladeshi businesses have no website or an outdated one. Pitch: "I can help you show up when customers search on Google."

      Step 5: Price sensibly. Beginners often charge ৳5,000–15,000 per month for local SEO retainers, or $50–200 per month on international platforms. Raise rates as results improve — our freelance pricing guide shows how to set rates without undercharging.

      SEO pairs well with other income streams too: your SEO skills grow your own sites while you serve clients.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      How much does SEO cost in Bangladesh?

      Doing it yourself costs nothing but time. Freelancers in Bangladesh typically charge ৳5,000–30,000 per month depending on scope; agencies charge more. For a small business, learning the basics and publishing helpful content is the most cost-effective start.

      Is SEO hard to learn for beginners?

      The basics are common sense: answer real questions clearly, use descriptive titles, earn links from other sites. The hard part is consistency and patience, not technical complexity. Most people can learn enough to rank a small site in 2–3 months of focused practice.

      Can I do SEO without a website?

      You can learn the concepts, but you need a website to practice. A simple WordPress blog costs very little — or practice on a friend's or family member's business site.

      Does SEO work for Bangla-language content?

      Yes. Google indexes and ranks Bangla content well, and competition in Bangla is often much lower than in English. Many niches have almost no quality Bangla content — which means ranking is easier for those who publish it.

      SEO vs Facebook ads — which is better for a small business?

      Facebook ads bring fast, paid traffic — good for quick sales. SEO builds free, long-term traffic. Most smart businesses eventually use both. With zero budget, SEO costs nothing but your time.

      How do I know my SEO is working?

      Track three things: (1) rankings for target keywords, (2) organic traffic in Google Search Console, and (3) real business results — calls, messages, sales.

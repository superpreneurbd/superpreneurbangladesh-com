About

Imtiaz Khan

Imtiaz Khan is a digital marketer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. He is the founder of two ventures: Expert Asset Studio, an execution partner for small agencies, and Superpreneur Bangladesh, an education platform that helps Bangladeshi professionals build a second income.

Expert Asset Studio

Expert Asset Studio works behind the scenes as an execution partner for small agencies. Instead of strategy decks and advice, the studio handles implementation — the actual work of getting digital marketing done. Services include paid advertising, landing pages, video editing, marketing copywriting, conversion rate optimisation (CRO), and automation.

Agencies interested in execution support: see how hiring works or email imtiaz@superpreneur.bd.

Superpreneur Bangladesh

Superpreneur Bangladesh is built for a specific audience: full-time employed people — bankers, teachers, HR professionals, merchandisers and others — who want to build a second income without quitting their jobs. The Bangla site lives at superpreneur.bd; this English site serves the diaspora and global readers. Free video tutorials run on the Superpreneur Bangladesh YouTube channel.

The approach

No guaranteed-income promises. No secret formulas. Just honest, practical guidance: which skills pay, how long things really take, and what to avoid. The audience is people with jobs, salaries and responsibilities — a group most online-income content ignores.

SEO — search engine optimization — is the practice of helping websites rank higher on Google. In Bangladesh, SEO jobs exist at agencies, in-house at e-commerce companies and startups, and in the freelance/remote market serving foreign clients. It's a career you can enter without a degree, learn entirely online, and scale from a ৳25,000/month junior role to a six-figure consulting practice.

This is the roadmap I wish someone had given me.

Salary note: Ranges below are based on observed market listings in Bangladesh as of 2026, not a formal survey. Treat them as directional.

What SEO Specialists Actually Do

Forget the mystique. Day-to-day SEO work is:

  • Keyword research: Finding what people search for and mapping it to content
  • On-page optimization: Titles, headings, content structure, internal links
  • Technical SEO: Site speed, mobile issues, crawl errors, schema markup
  • Content strategy: Planning what to publish and why
  • Link building: Earning backlinks from other websites (the hardest part)
  • Reporting: Showing what changed and why it matters

About 70% of the job is unglamorous: audits, spreadsheets, and patient iteration. The people who love the detective work thrive; the people expecting magic tricks quit.

The Roadmap: Beginner to Expert

Phase 1: Foundations (Months 1-3)

Learn how search works. Not tactics — fundamentals.

  • How Google crawls, indexes, and ranks pages
  • What keywords are and how search intent works
  • On-page basics: title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure
  • Google Search Console and Google Analytics setup

Do this: Start a practice blog (free on WordPress.com or Blogger). Publish 10 articles targeting real keywords. Watch what ranks. This blog is your laboratory — every SEO expert learned by doing.

Free resources: Google's SEO Starter Guide, YouTube tutorials, and our SEO basics guide.

Phase 2: Technical Skills (Months 4-6)

  • Technical audits with free tools (Screaming Frog free version, Google PageSpeed Insights)
  • Schema markup basics (Article, FAQ, LocalBusiness)
  • Site speed optimization fundamentals
  • Understanding Core Web Vitals

Do this: Offer free SEO audits to 5 local businesses. You'll learn more from 5 real audits than from 50 tutorials, and 1-2 will hire you.

Phase 3: Content and Links (Months 7-12)

  • Content strategy: building topical clusters, not random articles
  • Internal linking architecture
  • Link building: guest posts, digital PR, resource link building
  • Competitive analysis: reverse-engineering what ranks

Do this: Get your first paying client or junior job. Even ৳15,000/month for part-time SEO work teaches you client management — a skill tutorials never cover.

Phase 4: Specialization (Year 2)

Pick a lane:

  • Technical SEO — highest pay, most complex, best for analytical minds
  • Content-led SEO — best for writers; combines SEO with content strategy
  • Local SEO — Google Business Profiles, local rankings; huge demand from Bangladeshi businesses
  • E-commerce SEO — product pages, category optimization; pays well because it ties directly to sales

Phase 5: Authority (Year 3+)

  • Consulting: charge for strategy, not hours
  • Build in public: write about SEO, share case studies (anonymized)
  • Remote clients: Bangladeshi SEO consultants serving foreign clients earn 3-5x local rates
  • Productize: SEO audits, courses, templates — turn expertise into assets

| Level | Experience | Typical Monthly Range |

|-------|-----------|----------------------|

| Junior SEO Executive | 0-1 year | ৳20,000–৳30,000 |

| SEO Specialist | 1-3 years | ৳30,000–৳55,000 |

| SEO Manager | 5+ years | ৳80,000–৳1,50,000 |

The freelance/remote column is where the real money is. A senior SEO specialist earning ৳70,000 locally can earn the equivalent of ৳2,00,000+ doing the same work for US or UK clients.

Where SEO Jobs Are

  • Agencies in Dhaka — the most common entry point; fast learning, modest pay
  • E-commerce companies — Daraz sellers, local D2C brands; SEO directly drives their revenue
  • SaaS and startups — smaller in Bangladesh, but growing
  • Freelance platforms — Upwork has constant SEO demand; build a profile around one specialty
  • Remote companies — the highest pay; requires proof (case studies, rankings you've achieved)

Tools to Learn (Free First)

  1. Google Search Console (free, essential)
  2. Google Analytics (free, essential)
  3. Google Keyword Planner (free with any Google account)
  4. Screaming Frog (free up to 500 URLs)
  5. Ubersuggest (free tier for keyword ideas)

    6. Paid tools (Ahrefs, Semrush) are powerful but unnecessary until you're earning from SEO. Don't buy tools before you have clients.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is SEO still relevant with AI search growing?

    Yes, but it's evolving. AI Overviews and chat-based search change how people find information, but businesses still need visibility wherever people look. SEOs who learn GEO (generative engine optimization) alongside traditional SEO will be the most valuable.

    How long does it take to learn SEO?

    Basics: 3 months of focused learning and practice. Job-ready: 6-12 months with a portfolio of real work. Expert: 3-5 years. SEO rewards patience — it's a compounding skill.

    Do I need to know coding for SEO?

    Basic HTML helps (title tags, headings, schema), but you don't need to be a developer. Understanding how websites work technically matters more than writing code.

    Can I learn SEO without a mentor?

    Yes. The SEO community shares knowledge freely — blogs, YouTube, Twitter/X. What you can't get from content is feedback on your work, which is why practice sites and free audits for real businesses matter so much.

    Should I specialize or stay generalist?

    Start generalist (learn all of SEO), then specialize around year 2. Specialists earn more because they're harder to replace.

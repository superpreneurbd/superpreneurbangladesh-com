You landed the client. You did the work. Now the money needs to reach your bank account in Bangladesh.

This is where many beginners get stuck. PayPal does not fully work here. Clients cannot just bKash you from London. But there are reliable methods — this guide covers all of them.

Why This Matters Before You Start

Set up your payment method BEFORE you need it. Account verification takes 3–7 days. If you win a client on Monday and start your Payoneer application on Tuesday, you look unprofessional when the client asks "how do I pay you?"

Do it this week, even if you have no clients yet.

Method 1: Payoneer (Most Popular)

Payoneer is the default choice for Bangladeshi freelancers. It integrates directly with Fiverr, Upwork, and most freelance platforms.

How it works

  1. Client or platform sends USD to your Payoneer account.
  2. You withdraw from Payoneer to your Bangladeshi bank account in BDT.
  3. Money arrives in 1–3 business days.

    4. Setup steps

    1. Go to Payoneer.com and sign up with your real name (must match your NID/passport).
    2. Choose "Freelancer" as your account type.
    3. Verify your identity with your NID, passport, or driving license.
    4. Add your Bangladeshi bank account (any major bank works — DBBL, Brac Bank, City Bank, etc.).
    5. Wait for approval (usually 2–5 business days).

      6. Fees to know

      • Receiving from Fiverr/Upwork: free or minimal.
      • Receiving from direct clients: around 3% via credit card, free via bank transfer to your Payoneer USD account details.
      • Withdrawing to Bangladeshi bank: around 2% above the mid-market exchange rate.
      • Annual account fee: applies if your account is inactive for 12 months.

      Pros and cons

      Pros: Works with every major platform. Virtual USD/EUR/GBP bank accounts included. Trusted by millions of freelancers.

      Cons: Exchange rate markup on withdrawals. Customer support can be slow.

      Method 2: Wise (Lowest Fees)

      Wise (formerly TransferWise) gives you the real exchange rate with a small transparent fee. For direct clients, it is often cheaper than Payoneer.

      How it works

      1. You share your Wise USD/EUR/GBP account details with the client.
      2. Client sends money like a local bank transfer in their country.
      3. You convert to BDT and withdraw to your Bangladeshi bank.

        4. Setup steps

        1. Sign up at Wise.com with your email.
        2. Verify your identity (NID or passport + selfie).
        3. Open a USD balance to get US bank details (routing + account number).
        4. Share those details with your client.
        5. When money arrives, convert to BDT and send to your bank.

          6. Fees to know

          • Receiving: free for most currencies.
          • Converting USD to BDT: approximately 0.5–1% (shown upfront before you confirm).
          • Sending to Bangladeshi bank: small fixed fee.

          Pros and cons

          Pros: Best exchange rates. Fully transparent fees — you see exactly what you get. Fast transfers.

          Cons: Not integrated with Fiverr/Upwork (use Payoneer there). Some clients have never heard of it.

          When to use Wise: Direct clients, especially those paying $500+ per invoice. The savings add up.

          Method 3: Direct Bank Transfer (SWIFT)

          For large payments ($2,000+), a SWIFT transfer straight to your Bangladeshi bank account works.

          How it works

          1. Give the client your bank's SWIFT code, your account number, and your full name.
          2. Client's bank sends an international wire.
          3. Your bank receives it in 3–5 business days and converts to BDT.

            4. Fees to know

            • Incoming wire fee: your bank charges around BDT 500–1,500.
            • Exchange rate: banks use their own rate, usually worse than Wise.
            • Intermediary bank fees: $15–$30 can be deducted in transit.

            When to use it

            Only for large, established client relationships. For amounts under $1,000, Payoneer or Wise is cheaper and faster.

            Method 4: Cryptocurrency (Use With Caution)

            Some international clients offer to pay in USDT or Bitcoin. Technically possible, but consider:

            • Bangladesh Bank has not authorized crypto transactions. The regulatory situation is unclear.
            • Converting crypto to BDT requires P2P trading, which carries fraud risk.
            • Price volatility can eat your earnings overnight.

            Our advice: Stick to Payoneer and Wise unless you fully understand the risks.

            What About PayPal?

            PayPal accounts from Bangladesh cannot receive payments fully. You can send money but not receive it into a Bangladeshi PayPal account. Some freelancers use workarounds (foreign accounts, friends abroad), but these violate PayPal's terms and risk frozen funds.

            Do not build your business on a workaround. Use Payoneer and Wise.

            Invoicing: Look Professional

            For direct clients, send a proper invoice. Include:

            • Your name and contact details
            • Client's name and company
            • Invoice number and date
            • Description of work delivered
            • Amount in USD (or agreed currency)
            • Payment details (your Payoneer/Wise account info)
            • Payment due date

            Free tools: Wave, Zoho Invoice, or even a clean Google Docs template. Professional invoices get paid faster.

            Tax Note

            Money you receive from abroad counts as income. Bangladesh offers tax incentives for freelancers bringing in foreign currency (historically a reduced rate for IT/freelance income — confirm current rules with a tax advisor). Keep records of every payment: platform statements, bank receipts, invoices.

            Recommended Setup for Beginners

            1. Open Payoneer now — covers Fiverr, Upwork, and most platforms.
            2. Open Wise next — covers direct clients at lower fees.
            3. Connect both to your Bangladeshi bank account.
            4. Test with a small amount before your first big payment arrives.

              5. Total cost to set up: zero. Both accounts are free to open.

              Frequently Asked Questions

              Which is better for Bangladesh, Payoneer or Wise?

              Use both. Payoneer for platform work (Fiverr, Upwork) because it integrates directly. Wise for direct clients because fees and exchange rates are better. There is no reason to choose only one — both are free to open.

              How long does it take to withdraw from Payoneer to a Bangladeshi bank?

              Usually 1–3 business days. First-time withdrawals can take slightly longer due to verification checks.

              Can I receive payments in bKash from international clients?

              Not directly. International clients cannot send to bKash. The workaround: receive via Payoneer/Wise to your bank, then transfer to bKash yourself. For local Bangladeshi clients, bKash and Nagad work perfectly.

              What documents do I need to verify Payoneer?

              Your NID (National ID), passport, or driving license. The name must match your Payoneer account exactly. You may also need a bank statement or utility bill for address verification.

              Is there a minimum withdrawal amount?

              Payoneer typically requires a $50 minimum for bank withdrawals. Wise has very low minimums (a few dollars). Plan accordingly if you do small gigs.

              Do I need a dollar account in a Bangladeshi bank?

              No. Payoneer and Wise convert to BDT and deposit into your regular BDT savings account.

If you earn money from clients abroad, one of your first decisions is where that money lands. For Bangladeshi freelancers, the two serious options are Payoneer and Wise (formerly TransferWise). Both let you receive international payments and withdraw to a Bangladeshi bank account — but they work differently, and the right choice depends on how you earn.

This guide compares Payoneer and Wise honestly, side by side, so you can pick based on your actual situation — not based on what worked for someone else.

What Each Service Actually Does

Payoneer is built for freelancers and businesses. It gives you "receiving accounts" — virtual bank accounts in the US, UK, EU, and other countries — so clients and marketplaces can pay you as if you had a local account there. You then withdraw the money to your Bangladeshi bank account.

Wise is built for international money transfers. It is famous for low, transparent fees and the real mid-market exchange rate. It also offers multi-currency accounts where you can hold balances in different currencies and get local account details in several countries.

Both are legitimate, established companies used by millions. Neither is a scam or a grey-market workaround — that matters, because your hard-earned money deserves a proper channel.

How Bangladeshi Freelancers Actually Get Paid

Before comparing features, it helps to know the three common ways money reaches you:

  1. From freelance marketplaces (Fiverr, Upwork): the marketplace holds your earnings, then you withdraw to your payment provider.
  2. From direct clients abroad: the client sends money to your virtual receiving account details, like a bank transfer in their own country.
  3. From digital product or service sales: platforms like Gumroad or your own checkout send payouts to your linked account.

    4. Payoneer connects directly to Fiverr and Upwork — this is its biggest practical advantage in Bangladesh. Wise does not have the same one-click marketplace integrations, though you can still receive client payments to your Wise account details.

    Side-by-Side Comparison

    Receiving accounts and currencies

    Payoneer gives you receiving accounts in several major currencies — USD, EUR, GBP, and others — with full account details (account number, sort code, IBAN) you can share with clients. Wise's multi-currency account also gives local details in multiple currencies, and lets you hold 40+ currencies in one place.

    For most Bangladeshi freelancers who earn in USD, both cover what you need.

    Marketplace integration

    This is Payoneer's strongest edge. Fiverr and Upwork both support direct withdrawal to Payoneer, which makes moving earnings fast and simple. If most of your income comes from these two platforms, Payoneer is the path of least resistance.

    Wise works fine for direct clients and for holding balances, but getting Fiverr or Upwork money into Wise involves more steps.

    Fees

    Both charge fees on different steps: receiving money, converting currency, and withdrawing to your bank.

    • Wise is known for showing the real exchange rate (the mid-market rate) and charging a small, clearly stated transfer fee. What you see is what you get.
    • Payoneer uses its own exchange rate when converting to BDT, which typically includes a margin, plus withdrawal fees. The cost structure is less transparent than Wise's, though Payoneer occasionally offers fee-free or discounted withdrawal promotions.

    Honest take: for pure cost on a straight currency transfer, Wise usually wins. For marketplace earnings where convenience matters, many freelancers accept Payoneer's slightly higher cost because the Fiverr/Upwork integration saves hassle.

    Withdrawal to Bangladeshi banks

    Both let you withdraw to Bangladeshi bank accounts in BDT. Transfers typically take one to three business days. Make sure the name on your payment account matches your bank account name exactly — mismatches cause delays and rejected transfers.

    Verification and account approval

    Both require identity verification: your national ID or passport, proof of address, and sometimes questions about how you earn. This is normal financial regulation, not a red flag.

    Practical tip: use the same name spelling everywhere — your ID, your payment account, and your bank account. Inconsistent spellings are the most common cause of stuck verifications.

    Support

    Both offer online support, but response quality varies. The freelancer community in Bangladesh has more collective experience with Payoneer — so when something goes wrong, you are more likely to find a Bangladeshi freelancer who has solved the same Payoneer problem. That community knowledge is worth something.

    Which One Should You Choose?

    Choose Payoneer if:

    • Most of your income comes from Fiverr or Upwork
    • You want receiving accounts to give to direct clients too
    • You value the one-click marketplace withdrawals

    Choose Wise if:

    • Most of your income comes from direct clients (not marketplaces)
    • You want the most transparent, usually lowest-cost currency conversion
    • You want to hold balances in multiple currencies

    Use both if: you have mixed income — Payoneer for marketplace earnings, Wise for direct clients and transfers. Many experienced freelancers end up with both, and there is no rule against it.

    Getting Set Up (The Practical Checklist)

    Regardless of which you choose, the setup process is similar:

    1. Register with your real details. Use your legal name exactly as it appears on your ID.
    2. Verify your identity. Upload a clear photo of your NID or passport. Blurry photos get rejected — take it in good light.
    3. Link your Bangladeshi bank account. Double-check the account number. A wrong digit means the money goes nowhere (or worse, somewhere wrong).
    4. Test with a small amount first. Before routing a big payment, withdraw a small sum to confirm everything works end to end.
    5. Keep records. Save every receipt and statement. You will need them for tax filing and for your own bookkeeping.

      6. Common Mistakes Bangladeshi Freelancers Make

      • Using someone else's account. Never route your earnings through a friend's Payoneer or Wise. If their account gets flagged, your money is trapped — and account sharing violates the terms of service.
      • Ignoring the exchange rate. The difference between a good and bad conversion rate adds up fast over a year. Compare what actually lands in your bank, not what the fee page advertises.
      • Letting money sit too long. Currency rates move. Decide your withdrawal rhythm (weekly, monthly) and stick to it instead of timing the market.
      • Not separating business money. Open a separate bank account for freelance income if you can. Mixing it with personal spending makes tracking impossible.

      What About Other Options?

      You may hear about other services — Skrill, PayPal (limited in Bangladesh), direct SWIFT transfers, or crypto. For most Bangladeshi freelancers in 2026, Payoneer and Wise remain the two reliable, well-supported choices. Direct bank SWIFT transfers work but usually cost more and take longer. Avoid any "agent" who offers to receive your payments for a cut — that is how money gets stolen.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Is Payoneer legal in Bangladesh?

      Yes. Payoneer is a regulated financial services company, and receiving freelance income through it is legal. You are still responsible for declaring your income and paying any applicable taxes — see our [tax guide for freelancers](/tax-guide-for-freelancers-bangladesh.html).

      Can I use Wise in Bangladesh?

      Yes. Wise supports Bangladesh — you can open an account, receive money in multiple currencies, and withdraw to Bangladeshi bank accounts. Availability of specific features can change, so check Wise's current supported-countries list when you sign up.

      Which is cheaper: Payoneer or Wise?

      For a straight currency transfer, Wise is usually cheaper because it uses the real mid-market rate with a transparent fee. Payoneer's conversion includes a margin. But total cost depends on your route — marketplace withdrawals via Payoneer can be worth the extra cost for the convenience.

      Can I have both Payoneer and Wise accounts?

      Yes. Many freelancers use Payoneer for Fiverr/Upwork withdrawals and Wise for direct clients. There is no restriction on having both.

      How long does withdrawal to a Bangladeshi bank take?

      Typically one to three business days for both services, assuming your details are correct and verification is complete. First withdrawals can take longer.

      Do I need a dollar account at my bank?

      No. Both services convert to BDT and deposit to your regular Bangladeshi bank account.

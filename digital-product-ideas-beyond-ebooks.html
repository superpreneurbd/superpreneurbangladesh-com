Ebooks are a great first digital product. But they are not the only one - and for many creators, they are not even the most profitable one.

Digital products share a beautiful trait: you create them once and sell them infinitely. No inventory, no shipping, no per-unit cost. The ideas below range from weekend projects to serious businesses. All are proven sellers.

Why Diversify Beyond Ebooks?

Different buyers prefer different formats. Some people love reading; others want templates they can use immediately, or tools that save them time. Multiple formats reach more buyers.

Higher price points. Many digital products sell for more than ebooks. A well-designed Notion template system can sell for ৳999-৳1,999 - double or triple a typical ebook price.

Faster to create. Some products on this list take a weekend to build, versus a month for an ebook. Faster creation means faster revenue and faster learning.

Portfolio effect. Multiple products cross-promote each other. A buyer of your template pack discovers your ebook. An ebook reader buys your planner. Each product makes the others more valuable.

The 15 Ideas

1. Notion Templates and Systems

What: Pre-built Notion workspaces for specific purposes: freelancers' client management, content calendars, budget trackers, project dashboards, habit trackers.

Why it works: Notion has millions of users who would rather buy a proven system than build from scratch. Templates solve the "blank page" problem.

Difficulty: Low-Medium. If you use Notion yourself, you can package your own setup.

Price range: ৳499-৳1,999 (international: $19-$79)

2. Printable Planners and Organizers

What: PDF planners people print at home: daily planners, budget planners, meal planners, wedding planners, study planners, business planners.

Why it works: Enormous demand, especially among students, professionals, and homemakers. Buyers print fresh copies as needed - one purchase, endless use.

Difficulty: Low. Canva has planner templates; customize and sell.

Price range: ৳199-৳599 (international: $5-$20)

3. Social Media Template Packs

What: Canva templates for Instagram posts, stories, LinkedIn carousels, YouTube thumbnails, Facebook ads. Organized by niche (fitness coaches, real estate, restaurants).

Why it works: Small businesses and creators need constant content but lack design skills. Template packs save them hours weekly.

Difficulty: Low-Medium. Requires basic Canva skills and understanding of what looks good.

Price range: ৳499-৳1,499 (international: $27-$97)

4. Resume and CV Templates

What: Professional, ATS-friendly resume templates in Word, Google Docs, and Canva formats. Include multiple designs and a guide on filling them out.

Why it works: Job seekers urgently need this and will pay for quality. High emotional stakes (getting hired) justify the price.

Difficulty: Low. Design skills help, but clean and simple wins.

Price range: ৳299-৳799 (international: $10-$30)

5. Proposal and Contract Templates

What: Ready-to-use freelancing proposals, client contracts, invoices, scope-of-work documents. Customizable for different industries.

Why it works: Freelancers know they need professional documents but dread creating them from scratch. Templates remove the friction.

Difficulty: Low. If you freelance, package what you already use.

Price range: ৳499-৳1,499 (international: $29-$99)

6. Lightroom Presets and Photo Filters

What: One-click photo editing presets for Adobe Lightroom (mobile and desktop). Organized by style: moody, bright and airy, vintage, professional headshots.

Why it works: Creators, influencers, and small businesses want consistent, professional-looking photos without learning editing.

Difficulty: Medium. Requires Lightroom knowledge and a good eye for color grading.

Price range: ৳399-৳1,299 (international: $15-$50)

7. Spreadsheet Templates and Calculators

What: Google Sheets or Excel templates: budget calculators, freelancing rate calculators, investment trackers, business profit dashboards, content calendars.

Why it works: Spreadsheets with built-in formulas save hours and prevent errors. Business buyers especially value these.

Difficulty: Low-Medium. Requires spreadsheet formula skills.

Price range: ৳299-৳999 (international: $10-$40)

8. Email Templates and Sequences

What: Done-for-you email templates: cold outreach sequences, welcome series, sales sequences, newsletter templates, client onboarding emails.

Why it works: Writing emails from scratch is painful. Templates give buyers a proven starting point they can customize.

Difficulty: Low. If you can write decent emails, you can package them.

Price range: ৳499-৳1,499 (international: $27-$97)

9. Website Themes and UI Kits

What: Pre-designed website templates for WordPress, or UI kits for designers (Figma components, landing page templates).

Why it works: Businesses need websites fast. A quality template saves weeks of design work.

Difficulty: High. Requires real design and development skills.

Price range: ৳1,999-৳9,999 (international: $49-$200)

10. Stock Photos and Graphics Packs

What: Curated photo collections for specific niches (Bangladeshi lifestyle, office scenes, food photography), or graphic element packs (icons, illustrations, backgrounds).

Why it works: Content creators constantly need fresh visuals. Niche-specific packs (especially local/Bangladeshi content) have less competition.

Difficulty: Medium. Requires photography or illustration skills.

Price range: ৳499-৳1,999 (international: $20-$80)

11. Online Workshops and Masterclasses

What: Live or recorded 2-3 hour deep dives on specific topics. More focused than a full course, more interactive than an ebook.

Why it works: Lower commitment than a course, higher perceived value than an ebook. Live versions create urgency.

Difficulty: Medium. Requires presentation skills and topic mastery.

Price range: ৳999-৳2,999 (international: $29-$99)

12. Membership Communities

What: Paid private communities (Facebook Group, Discord, WhatsApp) offering ongoing value: monthly Q&As, exclusive content, networking, accountability.

Why it works: Recurring revenue. Members pay monthly or yearly for continued access. Community itself becomes the product.

Difficulty: Medium-High. Requires consistent engagement and content.

Price range: ৳299-৳999/month (international: $10-$50/month)

13. Digital Art and Wall Prints

What: Printable wall art: motivational quotes, Islamic calligraphy, minimalist designs, nursery prints, office decor.

Why it works: Buyers print at local shops and frame. Huge gift market. Islamic art has strong demand in Bangladesh with limited quality supply.

Difficulty: Low-Medium. Canva plus good taste goes far.

Price range: ৳149-৳499 (international: $5-$15)

14. Language Learning Resources

What: Vocabulary flashcard decks, grammar cheat sheets, conversation practice guides, pronunciation audio packs. English-for-Bangladeshis is a massive market.

Why it works: Enormous, evergreen demand. English proficiency directly affects income in Bangladesh, so buyers are motivated.

Difficulty: Low-Medium. Requires language teaching knowledge.

Price range: ৳299-৳999 (international: $10-$40)

15. Business SOP Templates

What: Standard Operating Procedure templates for small businesses: hiring checklists, onboarding flows, customer service scripts, inventory management sheets.

Why it works: Small business owners know they need systems but do not know where to start. Templates give them a professional framework instantly.

Difficulty: Medium. Requires business operations knowledge.

Price range: ৳999-৳2,999 (international: $49-$149)

Choosing Your Next Product

Do not try to build all 15. Pick using this framework:

1. What do you already know? Your existing skills determine your fastest path. A designer should make template packs; a freelancer should make proposal templates.

2. What does your audience ask for? Look at questions you receive repeatedly. Each repeated question is a product idea.

3. What is the effort-to-revenue ratio? A weekend template pack that sells for ৳799 often outperforms a month-long ebook selling for ৳499 in hourly return.

4. What builds on what you have? If you wrote a freelancing ebook, proposal templates are the natural next product. Same audience, complementary value.

Where to Sell Digital Products

The platforms from our ebook selling guide apply here too, with adjustments:

  • Gumroad: Excellent for all digital product types. Built-in delivery, affiliate program, simple setup.
  • Etsy: Strong for printables, planners, and art. Huge built-in marketplace traffic.
  • Your own website: Best margins, full control. Use WooCommerce with digital download support.
  • Creative Market: For design assets (templates, graphics, fonts). Curated marketplace with quality buyers.
  • Social direct (bKash/Nagad): Works for all product types in Bangladesh. Start here to validate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which digital product is most profitable for beginners?

Template packs (social media, Notion, resume) have the best effort-to-revenue ratio for beginners. They are fast to create, easy to understand, and have clear value.

Do I need a large audience to sell digital products?

No, but it helps. Marketplaces (Etsy, Creative Market) provide built-in traffic. For direct sales, even 500 engaged followers can generate meaningful revenue if the product fits their needs.

How do I protect my digital products from piracy?

You cannot fully prevent it. Focus on: watermarking previews (not full products), reasonable pricing (piracy drops when prices feel fair), and building a brand people want to support. Most creators find piracy is a smaller problem than feared.

Should I offer bundles?

Yes. Bundling related products increases average order value significantly. "Buy the template pack + ebook together and save 30%" is a proven offer structure.

Can I sell the same product on multiple platforms?

Generally yes. Check each platform's exclusivity terms (most do not require exclusivity for digital products).

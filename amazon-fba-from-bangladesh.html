You have seen the videos. Someone in Dhaka claims they built a seven-figure Amazon business from their bedroom. So you are wondering: can I really do Amazon FBA from Bangladesh?

Here is the honest answer: technically, yes — it is possible. But it is much harder from Bangladesh than the YouTube gurus admit. There are real barriers around company registration, banking, capital, and logistics that most people are not prepared for.

This article gives you the full picture. No hype. Just the real barriers, the realistic paths that actually work, and a decision framework so you can decide whether this is for you — or whether your time and money are better spent elsewhere.

What Amazon FBA Actually Is

FBA stands for "Fulfillment by Amazon." Here is how it works in simple terms:

  1. You find a product to sell (kitchen tools, phone accessories, pet supplies — things like that).
  2. You buy it in bulk from a manufacturer, usually in China.
  3. You ship the inventory to Amazon's warehouses in the US, UK, or Europe.
  4. When a customer orders, Amazon stores, packs, ships, and handles customer service.
  5. You manage the business: product research, listing optimization, ads, and restocking.

    6. Amazon does the hard physical work. You do the thinking work. That is why it appeals to people — it looks like a hands-off online business.

    The catch: everything before step 4 is where the difficulty lives, especially from Bangladesh.

    The Real Barriers from Bangladesh

    Barrier 1: Amazon Has No Bangladesh Marketplace

    Amazon does not operate in Bangladesh. There is no amazon.com.bd. You cannot sell to Bangladeshi customers on Amazon.

    That means you must sell on Amazon US, UK, Canada, or a European marketplace. Those marketplaces are the most competitive on the planet. You will compete against sellers from the US, China, and Europe who have been doing this for years.

    Barrier 2: You Need a Foreign Company or Partner

    To open an Amazon seller account for the US marketplace, Amazon wants:

    • A registered business (an LLC in the US is the common route)
    • A US bank account to receive payments
    • A tax ID (EIN in the US)

    Getting an LLC from Bangladesh is possible through online formation services, but it costs money (often $200–500+ per year for a registered agent and filings) and adds paperwork. Some sellers work with a partner or relative abroad who already has a company and bank account. That works, but it creates dependence — your business runs through someone else's identity.

    Amazon also verifies identities strictly. Getting your seller account approved from a Bangladeshi IP with foreign documents raises flags. Many Bangladeshi sellers get stuck at verification for weeks or months.

    Barrier 3: Capital Requirements Are Real

    This is the barrier most gurus skip. FBA is not a zero-capital business. Realistic costs to launch one product:

    • Product inventory: $3,000–$10,000 for a first order
    • Shipping to Amazon warehouses: $1,000–$3,000 (sea freight from China)
    • Amazon fees (referral fees, FBA fees, storage): roughly 30–40% of your sale price
    • Product photography, samples, and branding: $500–$1,500
    • Amazon PPC ads to launch: $500–$2,000+
    • LLC formation and registered agent: $200–$500/year

    A realistic minimum to start FBA properly is BDT 5–10 lakh ($4,000–$8,000). Anyone telling you that you can start with BDT 20,000 is selling you a course, not a business plan.

    Barrier 4: Payment and Tax Complications

    Amazon pays you in the marketplace's currency (USD for Amazon US). To get that money to Bangladesh, you need a US bank account or a service like Payoneer or a US LLC bank account. Then you transfer to Bangladesh, dealing with conversion fees and Bangladeshi banking rules on foreign income.

    On taxes: selling in the US means dealing with US tax requirements. Many foreign sellers need to file US tax returns for their LLC even if they owe nothing. This is manageable with an accountant, but it is another cost and another thing that can go wrong if ignored.

    For more on handling international payments as a Bangladeshi, read our guide on receiving international payments.

    The Realistic Paths That Actually Work

    If you still want to pursue Amazon after reading the barriers, here are the paths with the best odds:

    Path 1: Partner with Someone Abroad

    If you have a trusted relative or friend in the US, UK, or Canada, they can open the seller account and bank account while you handle product research, listing, and ads from Bangladesh. This removes the biggest paperwork barrier.

    Honest warning: partnerships involving money and family can go bad. Put everything in writing. Agree on profit splits, who pays for inventory, and what happens if one of you wants out.

    Path 2: Start with Amazon KDP Instead

    Amazon KDP (Kindle Direct Publishing) is Amazon's ebook and print-on-demand platform. The barriers are dramatically lower:

    • No inventory, no shipping, no warehouse fees
    • No company registration needed in most cases
    • You can start with near-zero capital — just your time and a laptop
    • Amazon pays royalties directly through services available in Bangladesh

    You write (or commission) books — low-content books like planners, journals, and notebooks are popular — publish them, and earn royalties per sale. It is not easy money; the KDP market is competitive and low-quality books earn nothing. But the barrier to start is genuinely low.

    This fits well with the "Salary to Second Income" idea I teach at Superpreneur Bangladesh — turning what you already know into digital products. An ebook on Amazon KDP is a digital product with a global storefront built in.

    Path 3: Build Capital First Through Freelancing

    This is the path I recommend most. Instead of borrowing or risking BDT 5–10 lakh on a business model you have never tried:

    1. Spend 6–12 months freelancing and save the capital you will need.
    2. Learn digital marketing, product research, and advertising — the exact skills FBA requires.
    3. Then start FBA (or any business) with money you earned and skills you tested on clients.

      4. If you go this route, start with our freelancing guide for Bangladeshis and our freelance pricing guide.

      Path 4: Start with Domestic E-commerce Instead

      Honestly, for most Bangladeshis, selling on Facebook (f-commerce) or importing small quantities from China to sell locally is a smarter first business than Amazon FBA. The capital requirements are lower, the logistics are simpler, and you understand your own market.

      Read our guides on selling on Facebook in Bangladesh and importing from China for the full picture.

      Decision Framework: Should You Attempt Amazon FBA?

      You SHOULD consider it if:

      • You have BDT 5–10 lakh you can afford to lose completely (treat it as tuition, not savings)
      • You have a trusted partner abroad OR you can set up a US LLC properly
      • You already understand digital marketing, product research, or e-commerce
      • You can commit 10–15 hours per week for at least a year

      You should NOT attempt it if:

      • You are borrowing the money or using your family's emergency savings
      • You have no marketing or e-commerce experience and no capital to hire help
      • You expect profit in 2–3 months (realistic timeline: 6–12 months to know if a product works)
      • Someone's course is your main source of information and they promised easy results

      If you checked more boxes in the second list, you are not a failure — you are just not ready yet. Build skills and capital first. The opportunity will still be there in a year.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Can I open an Amazon seller account from Bangladesh?

      You can register on Amazon's seller platform from Bangladesh, but selling on Amazon US/UK requires a bank account and tax information from those countries. Bangladeshi bank accounts and NID documents are generally not accepted for the major marketplaces. Most Bangladeshi sellers solve this with a US LLC + US bank account or a partner abroad.

      How much money do I need to start Amazon FBA from Bangladesh?

      A realistic minimum is BDT 5–10 lakh ($4,000–$8,000) for one product, covering inventory, shipping, Amazon fees, ads, photography, and company setup. Starting with less usually means you run out of money before the product has a chance to sell.

      Is Amazon FBA profitable in 2026?

      It can be, for sellers who pick the right product and execute well. But the "easy money" era is over. Competition is intense, Amazon's fees take a large cut, and ad costs keep rising. Many beginners lose money on their first product. Treat your first product as paid learning.

      What about Amazon KDP — is it easier from Bangladesh?

      Yes, significantly. KDP has no inventory, no warehouse fees, and no company registration requirement in most cases. You publish ebooks or print-on-demand books and earn royalties. The barrier is low, but the market is crowded — you need genuinely useful or well-designed books to earn meaningful income.

      Can I sell on Amazon without a company?

      For Amazon US as a foreign seller, Amazon generally requires business and tax documentation. Some sellers start as individuals, but a registered business (like a US LLC) is the standard and more reliable route, especially for payment and tax handling.

      What is the biggest mistake Bangladeshis make with Amazon FBA?

      Underestimating the capital needed and overestimating how fast profits come. The second biggest mistake is buying an expensive course before understanding the basics — learn the free fundamentals first, then decide if paid training is worth it. ## The Bottom Line Amazon FBA from Bangladesh is possible, but it is a capital-intensive, paperwork-heavy business — not the laptop-lifestyle shortcut it is sold as.

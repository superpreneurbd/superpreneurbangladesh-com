Pricing your ebook feels like guessing. Too high and nobody buys. Too low and you leave money on the table — or worse, people assume it is low quality.

The truth is, ebook pricing is part math, part psychology, and part testing. This guide gives you a framework to set a smart starting price and adjust it based on real data.

The Biggest Pricing Mistake: Underpricing

Most first-time authors price too low. They think "I am new, so I should charge less." This backfires for three reasons:

  1. Low prices signal low value. A ৳99 ebook feels disposable. A ৳499 ebook feels like an investment. Readers unconsciously link price to quality.
  2. You need fewer sales at higher prices. Selling 100 copies at ৳499 earns the same as selling 500 copies at ৳99 — but requires one-fifth the marketing effort.
  3. Discounts only work from a real price. You cannot run a "50% off sale" on a ৳99 ebook. Starting higher gives you promotional room.

    4. Unless you are deliberately using a free or ultra-cheap ebook as a lead magnet, do not race to the bottom.

    Pricing Framework: The 4 Factors

    1. Your Market's Purchasing Power

    Be honest about who is buying. A Bangladeshi job holder earning ৳40,000/month thinks differently about a ৳999 ebook than an American professional does about a $19 ebook.

    For the Bangladesh market, these are common working ranges for nonfiction ebooks:

    • Short guides (30–60 pages): ৳199–৳399
    • Full-length ebooks (80–150 pages): ৳399–৳799
    • Premium/specialized ebooks: ৳799–৳1,499

    For international markets (Amazon, Gumroad), the equivalent ranges are roughly $2.99–$4.99, $4.99–$9.99, and $9.99–$19.99.

    These are starting points, not rules. Your specific audience and topic matter more than any general range.

    2. The Transformation You Deliver

    Price follows perceived transformation. Ask: what is the reader's life like after reading?

    • An ebook that helps someone land a freelancing client worth ৳20,000 can easily justify ৳499.
    • An ebook of general motivation with no specific outcome struggles to justify even ৳199.

    The more concrete and valuable the outcome, the higher you can price. Before setting your price, write one sentence: "After reading my ebook, the reader will be able to ___." The bigger the blank, the higher the price.

    3. Comparable Books

    Look at what similar ebooks charge. Not to copy them, but to understand the market's expectations.

    Search your topic on Amazon, Gumroad, or local platforms. Note the prices of the top 5–10 results. If most cluster around $4.99, pricing yours at $29.99 needs strong justification — and pricing at $0.99 makes you look like the cheap option.

    Position yourself deliberately: slightly above average signals premium quality; slightly below average signals accessibility. Both are valid strategies.

    4. Your Business Model

    Your ebook's role in your business affects its price:

    • Lead magnet: Free or ৳99. The goal is email signups, not revenue.
    • Entry product: ৳299–৳499. Low enough for impulse buys, high enough to filter serious buyers.
    • Core product: ৳499–৳999. This is your main revenue driver.
    • Premium/flagship: ৳999+. Justified by depth, bonuses, or personal support included.

    Many successful authors use a ladder: a free chapter or cheap ebook leads to a mid-priced ebook, which leads to a course or coaching. Price each rung to make the next step feel natural.

    Pricing Psychology: Small Tricks That Work

    Charm Pricing

    Prices ending in 9 feel cheaper than round numbers. ৳499 feels significantly less than ৳500, even though the difference is ৳1. This is well-documented and works across markets.

    Anchor Pricing

    Show a higher "original" price next to your actual price. "৳999 → ৳499 (launch price)" makes ৳499 feel like a deal. The anchor must be believable — a fake inflated price destroys trust if discovered.

    Tiered Pricing

    Offer two or three versions:

    • Basic: Ebook only — ৳399
    • Plus: Ebook + worksheets/templates — ৳599
    • Premium: Ebook + templates + email support — ৳999

    Most buyers choose the middle option. Tiers increase average revenue per customer without extra marketing.

    The Decoy Effect

    The premium tier partly exists to make the middle tier look reasonable. Even if few people buy premium, its presence lifts middle-tier sales.

    Testing Your Price

    Do not treat your first price as permanent. Test it.

    How to Test

    1. Start at your best-guess price. Use the framework above.
    2. Run it for 2–4 weeks. You need enough traffic for meaningful data (at least 500–1,000 page views).
    3. Change one thing. Raise or lower by 20–30%. Do not change the sales page at the same time.
    4. Compare conversion rates AND total revenue. A lower price with more sales can earn less total than a higher price with fewer sales.

      5. What the Data Tells You

      • High traffic, low sales: Price might be too high — or your sales page needs work. Lower the price first (easier test).
      • Good conversion but you want more revenue: Try raising the price 20%. If conversion holds, keep it.
      • Selling well at current price: Raise it 10–15% and watch. Most authors discover they were underpriced.

      When to Discount (And When Not To)

      Good Reasons to Discount

      • Launch week: Reward early buyers and generate initial reviews.
      • Seasonal sales: Eid, New Year, Black Friday — readers expect deals.
      • Bundle offers: Discount when bought with another product.
      • Clearing old editions: Before releasing a new version.

      Bad Reasons to Discount

      • Panic: "Nobody is buying, let me cut the price in half." Fix your marketing first.
      • Permanently: A book that is always "on sale" trains readers to never pay full price.
      • Below your costs: If platform fees eat your margin, the sale loses money.

      Pricing for Different Platforms

      Amazon KDP

      Amazon's royalty structure affects pricing: ebooks priced $2.99–$9.99 earn 70% royalty; outside that range earns 35%. Price within $2.99–$9.99 unless you have a strategic reason not to.

      Gumroad / Direct Sales

      You control everything. Gumroad takes a flat fee plus a percentage. Factor this into your price — a ৳299 ebook nets less than you think after fees.

      Local Platforms (Bangladesh)

      Check each platform's commission before pricing. Some take 20–30%. Price so that your net earnings per sale make the effort worthwhile.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Should my first ebook be free?

      Only if it serves a clear purpose: building your email list or proving your expertise. A free ebook with no follow-up plan is just unpaid work. If you want revenue, charge from day one.

      What if competitors charge less than me?

      Compete on positioning, not price. A higher price with better testimonials, a stronger promise, and more specific outcomes wins over a cheaper generic alternative. Never start a price war you cannot win.

      How often should I change my price?

      Test quarterly, not weekly. Constant price changes confuse returning visitors and make promotions meaningless. Pick a price, commit for a season, then evaluate.

      Does a higher price mean fewer refunds?

      Generally, yes. Buyers who pay more are more committed to actually reading and applying the content. Ultra-cheap ebooks have higher refund rates because the purchase was impulsive.

      Should I price differently for Bangladesh vs international buyers?

      If you sell on your own website, you can use purchasing-power pricing (lower prices for Bangladeshi buyers). On Amazon, one global price applies.

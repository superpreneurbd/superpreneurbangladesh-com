Freelancing in the UK as a Bangladeshi: Tax, Payments & Getting Started

Written by Imtiaz KhanOctober 2026

Thousands of Bangladeshis live and work in the UK — in London, Birmingham, Manchester and beyond. Many want freelance income: students topping up their budget, professionals building a second stream, newcomers finding their footing. This guide covers the three things that matter most: staying legal on your visa, handling tax, and getting paid.

Important: this is general guidance, not legal or immigration advice. Visa rules and tax thresholds change. Always check the current UKVI guidance, your BRP conditions, and HMRC's website — or speak to a qualified adviser — before acting.

1. Visa rules come first

Your right to do freelance work in the UK depends entirely on your immigration status:

  • British citizens and settled status: you can freelance freely.
  • Student visa: you can usually work limited hours during term time, but self-employment is generally not permitted on a student visa. This is the rule most students get wrong — check your BRP and your university's immigration adviser.
  • Skilled Worker visa: you are tied to your sponsoring employer; freelance work outside it is restricted.
  • Graduate visa: much more flexible — most kinds of work, including self-employment, are allowed.

Bottom line: never start freelancing until you have confirmed your specific visa allows it. A quick check with your university adviser or an immigration solicitor is worth far more than this article.

2. Tax basics: Self Assessment

If you are allowed to freelance and your earnings cross the threshold, the UK system is straightforward:

  1. Register as self-employed with HMRC (as a sole trader). It is free and done online.
  2. Keep records of every invoice, payment and business expense from day one. A simple spreadsheet is enough at the start.
  3. File a Self Assessment tax return each year and pay what you owe. The personal allowance means you do not pay income tax below a certain level — check HMRC for the current figure.
  4. National Insurance may also apply depending on your profits.

Most beginners start as a sole trader. A limited company can make sense later as income grows — that is a conversation for an accountant.

3. Receiving payments in the UK

  • UK bank account: fine for UK clients paying in pounds.
  • Wise: the best all-rounder for international clients — multi-currency accounts, good rates, a proper UK account number and sort code.
  • Payoneer: useful if you also take work through Fiverr or Upwork, or invoice US companies.

If you also send money home to Bangladesh, Wise is usually the cheapest corridor for GBP to BDT. Full comparison: how Bangladeshi freelancers get paid.

4. Finding clients from the UK

Your position is genuinely strong. You understand the UK market and you can connect clients with skilled Bangladeshi talent at sensible rates — agencies pay attention to that combination.

  • Upwork and Fiverr: the standard starting point, same as everywhere.
  • LinkedIn: increasingly the best channel for direct UK clients. A clear headline about what you do beats a clever one.
  • Your community: Bangladeshi business owners across the UK need websites, ads and content. Your network is a client list waiting to happen.

Frequently asked questions

Can a Bangladeshi student in the UK do freelance work?
It depends on your visa conditions. UK student visas typically allow limited work hours during term time, but self-employment rules are stricter and depend on your specific visa. Always check your BRP conditions and the current UKVI guidance — or speak to your university's immigration adviser — before taking freelance work.
Do I need to pay tax on freelance income in the UK?
Generally yes. If you earn above the personal allowance through self-employment, you usually need to register with HMRC and file a Self Assessment tax return. Keep records of all income and expenses from day one.
What is the best way to receive freelance payments in the UK?
A UK bank account works for most clients. For international clients paying in other currencies, Wise offers multi-currency accounts with good exchange rates. Payoneer is useful if you also work through Fiverr or Upwork.
How do Bangladeshis in the UK find freelance clients?
The same platforms work everywhere: Upwork, Fiverr, and increasingly LinkedIn for direct clients. Your advantage is real: you understand both the UK market and the Bangladeshi talent pool, which agencies value.
Should I register as self-employed or set up a limited company?
Most beginners start as a sole trader — it is simpler and free to register with HMRC. A limited company can make sense later as income grows. This is a common question for an accountant, not something to decide from a blog post alone.

Getting Paid as a Freelancer in Bangladesh (2026)

Getting paid is the part of freelancing that worries beginners most — and it is the part with the clearest answer. Bangladeshi freelancers have reliable, well-tested ways to receive money from anywhere in the world. This guide compares Payoneer, Wise, bKash and bank transfer: what each costs, how fast it is, and which to use in each situation.

The short answer

  • International clients (Fiverr, Upwork): use Payoneer. It plugs directly into both platforms.
  • Direct international clients: use Wise for the best exchange rates, or Payoneer.
  • Local Bangladeshi clients: use bKash or bank transfer. Instant and simple.

Method-by-method comparison

Setting up Payoneer (the standard route)

  1. Create a free Payoneer account in your own legal name — it must match your bank account and national ID.
  2. Connect it to Fiverr and Upwork in each platform's payout settings.
  3. Add your Bangladeshi bank account as the withdrawal destination.
  4. Withdraw. First withdrawals can take a little longer while the account is verified.

Keep records of every payment — date, client, amount. You will need them for taxes and for your own bookkeeping.

When Wise wins

If a client pays you directly (not through a marketplace), Wise usually gives you more taka per dollar than Payoneer or a bank wire. You can also hold balances in USD, GBP and EUR and convert when the rate looks good. For Bangladeshis living abroad — students in the UK, workers in the Gulf — Wise is often the simplest way to move money home too.

Safety rules

  • Never share your bKash PIN, OTP codes, or bank passwords with anyone. No real client or platform ever asks for them.
  • Be suspicious of clients who insist on unusual payment methods — gift cards, crypto wallets you did not agree to, or "overpayments" they ask you to refund.
  • On marketplaces, keep communication and payment inside the platform until trust is established. Going off-platform early removes your protection.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best way for Bangladeshi freelancers to receive international payments?
Payoneer is the most widely used — it connects directly to Fiverr and Upwork and lets you withdraw to a Bangladeshi bank account. Wise is a strong alternative with competitive exchange rates and multi-currency accounts.
Is Payoneer free to use in Bangladesh?
Creating a Payoneer account is free. Payoneer charges fees on withdrawals and currency conversion, so check the current fee schedule inside your account before moving large amounts.
Can freelancers in Bangladesh receive payments through bKash?
bKash works well for local clients inside Bangladesh — it is instant and everyone has it. For international clients, you need Payoneer or Wise first, then you can move money to your local bank.
How long does it take to receive freelance payments in Bangladesh?
Marketplaces like Fiverr and Upwork hold funds for a clearance period (typically around 1–2 weeks for new sellers) before you can withdraw. After that, Payoneer or Wise transfers to a Bangladeshi bank usually take 1–3 business days.
Do I need a bank account to freelance from Bangladesh?
For international platforms, yes — Payoneer and Wise both withdraw to a local bank account in your name. For local clients, bKash or mobile banking is enough to start.

How to Sell Ebooks from Bangladesh Without Stripe or PayPal (2026)

IK
by Imtiaz Khan
Founder, Superpreneur Bangladesh
Updated October 2026
Free guides, no hype. We fund this site through our own ebooks, courses and services — never paid placements.

You cannot open a Stripe account as a Bangladesh-based seller, and a Bangladeshi PayPal account cannot receive or withdraw money. Neither fact has to stop you. Bangladeshi authors sell ebooks worldwide every day by using platforms that pay out to a local bank account or Payoneer, and by selling direct to local readers with bKash. This guide compares every route that actually works — fees, payout methods and all — so you can pick the one that fits your book and your buyers. It is the payments chapter of our digital product business guide.

The short answer

  • Global ebook sales: publish on Amazon KDP. Amazon pays royalties straight to your Bangladeshi bank account by transfer — no Stripe, no PayPal, no US company needed.
  • Bangladeshi readers: sell direct from your page or website and take bKash or Nagad. Instant payment, zero platform cut — this is how our own ebook business started.
  • Courses and other products: choose platforms that pay through Payoneer (for example Udemy), then withdraw to your local bank.
  • Direct international buyers: send a Payoneer or Wise payment request and deliver the file yourself — best for higher-priced products.
  • Gumroad: usable only if a bank-deposit payout is offered for your country — Gumroad does not pay out to Payoneer or Wise, so check your payout options before building on it.

Why Stripe and PayPal do not work from Bangladesh

Stripe simply does not onboard businesses registered in Bangladesh — there is no application to fill in, and accounts opened through workarounds get closed. PayPal is half-present: you can open an account and pay for things, but receiving payments and withdrawing to a Bangladeshi bank are not supported for Bangladesh-based accounts. Money sent to such an account can get stuck, which is worse than not using it at all.

The good news: the two services are checkout tools, not the internet's bank. Every route below replaces them with something that pays a Bangladeshi seller legally and reliably.

Route 1: Amazon KDP — the simplest global answer

If your ebook is in English (or aimed at the diaspora), KDP solves the whole problem in one account. You publish free, Amazon sells and delivers the book worldwide, and royalties arrive by bank transfer roughly 60 days after each month ends. Direct deposit has no minimum threshold; wire payments have one, so set up the bank details carefully and complete the tax interview honestly — a 30% US withholding applies to US sales unless a tax treaty rate applies to you, and the interview is where that gets sorted.

KDP also pairs well with the rest of this site: our guide on where to sell your ebook in Bangladesh compares KDP with the local options, and how to write your first ebook in 30 days gets the book itself finished.

Route 2: Sell direct with bKash and Nagad

For Bangladeshi readers, nothing beats direct sales. A buyer sees your Facebook post or video, pays with bKash, and gets the ebook — no platform takes a cut and the money is in your account in minutes. This is the model our own business runs on, and it funds everything else we do.

Two things make it work: a clear order process (a simple form or page that captures the buyer's email and payment number) and reliable delivery (email the file, or use an automated delivery tool once volume grows). Set up your receiving properly with our bKash for business guide and the Nagad merchant guide. The weakness is reach: bKash only works for buyers inside Bangladesh, which is why direct sales and KDP together cover almost everyone.

Route 3: Platforms that pay through Payoneer

Payoneer is the bridge most Bangladeshi online earners already use — it plugs into Fiverr and Upwork, and it is also how several product platforms pay out. Udemy pays instructors through Payoneer, so the same account that receives your freelance income can receive course income. From Payoneer you withdraw to your local bank in taka. Our getting paid guide compares Payoneer, Wise and bKash side by side, and how to receive international payments in Bangladesh covers the full setup. Rule of thumb: before joining any platform, check its payout page for the word "Payoneer" or "bank transfer". If the only options are Stripe and PayPal, that platform is closed to you — move on.

Route 4: Payment requests for direct international buyers

Selling a higher-priced product — a ৳5,000 course bundle, a coaching ebook package — to a handful of international buyers? Skip platforms entirely. Payoneer lets you request payment from a client, and Wise payment links let buyers pay by card or bank transfer at honest exchange rates. You then deliver the file by email. It is manual, which is exactly why it suits low-volume, high-value sales and fails for high-volume, low-price ones.

The advanced option: a US LLC plus Stripe

You will hear about this route: form a US company, open Stripe, sell like an American business. It works, and it is what some large sellers eventually do — but it costs real money every year (formation, registered agent, US tax filing) before you sell a single copy. Do the honest maths: until your monthly sales are comfortably in the thousands of dollars, the routes above keep more money in your pocket. Our own channel breaks this down in the video below.

Three mistakes that freeze accounts

Using someone else's PayPal. A cousin abroad "lending" an account sounds harmless until the account is limited for mismatched identity — with your balance inside.

Opening Stripe with a VPN and a borrowed address. Stripe verifies identity and tax details. Accounts built on fiction get closed at the first payout review, and the funds can be held for months.

Mismatched names. Your platform account, Payoneer account and bank account should all carry the same legal name. Most "missing payment" stories are really name-mismatch stories.

Platform payout options and fees change. Verify the payout methods inside your own account before you build a launch around any single route. This page is practical guidance, not financial or tax advice.

Frequently asked questions

Can I open a Stripe account in Bangladesh?
No. Stripe does not onboard businesses based in Bangladesh. The legal alternatives are a foreign company (for example a US LLC with its own tax filings) or — far cheaper for most sellers — the routes in this guide: KDP bank payouts, bKash direct sales, and Payoneer-paying platforms.
Can I receive money with PayPal in Bangladesh?
Not reliably. Bangladesh-based PayPal accounts cannot receive payments and withdraw to a local bank the way accounts in supported countries can. Do not build a business on receiving PayPal payments in Bangladesh; use Payoneer or bank-transfer platforms instead.
Does Gumroad work from Bangladesh?
Gumroad pays creators by bank deposit in supported countries or by PayPal, and it does not pay out to Payoneer or Wise. If a bank-deposit payout in BDT is offered in your Gumroad payout settings, it can work; if your only option is PayPal, it cannot. Check your own payout settings before relying on it.
How does Amazon KDP pay Bangladeshi authors?
By bank transfer (EFT or wire) to the bank account you add in your KDP settings, around 60 days after the end of the month in which royalties were earned. KDP also accepts payment-service accounts such as Payoneer, which is on Amazon's approved provider list. Complete the tax interview so withholding is applied correctly.
Can international buyers pay me with bKash?
No — bKash is a Bangladeshi domestic service. International buyers pay through the platform you sell on (KDP, a Payoneer-paying platform) or through a Payoneer/Wise payment request. Use bKash and Nagad for buyers inside Bangladesh, where they are instant and nearly free.
Do I need a trade licence or a US LLC to sell ebooks?
Not to start. You can publish on KDP and sell direct with bKash as an individual. As income grows, formalising (trade licence, proper tax records) becomes wise — keep every payout receipt from day one. A US LLC only makes financial sense at high, steady sales volume.

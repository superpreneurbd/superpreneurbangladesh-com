Someone in Dhaka types "emergency plumber near me" into Google. Someone in Chattogram searches "best IELTS coaching center." They are not scrolling for entertainment — they need something right now. Google Ads puts your business at the top at that exact moment. That intent is the fundamental difference from Facebook ads — and why every Bangladeshi business owner should understand search advertising.

How Google Ads Differs From Facebook Ads

The difference comes down to one word: intent.

  • Facebook ads = interruption. People scroll photos and videos; your ad interrupts them. Targeting is good — but nobody was looking to buy.
  • Google Ads = intent. People typed exactly what they want and are actively searching. These clicks convert better because the buyer came to you.

This means:

  • Google Ads wins when customers search for what you sell: plumbers, clinics, lawyers, coaching, hotels, B2B services, software.
  • Facebook ads wins when customers do not know they want your product yet: fashion, gadgets, cosmetics, impulse buys.

Many businesses use both. If you already run Facebook ads, Google Ads is the natural second channel — it captures buyers Facebook cannot reach.

On Google you pay per click — no click, no charge. On Facebook you often pay per impression.

When Should a Business Use Google Ads?

Google Ads is worth it when all three of these are true:

  1. People search for what you sell. Google your service with a Bangladesh location ("AC repair Dhaka"). If ads appear at the top, competitors are paying — proof the searches carry buying intent.
  2. Each customer is worth enough. A ৳500 sale with thin margins cannot survive expensive clicks; a ৳5,000–50,000 customer (coaching, medical, B2B) easily can.
  3. You can handle the inquiries. Ads are wasted if nobody answers the phone or replies to messages. Fix your follow-up before you spend.

    4. Typical winners in Bangladesh: coaching centers, clinics, home services (plumbing, AC repair), real estate, study-abroad consultancies, hotels, B2B suppliers, and IT services.

    Setting Up: Account, Campaigns, and Structure

    1. Create a Google Ads account at ads.google.com with your business email, setting currency to BDT and country to Bangladesh.
    2. Choose "Expert Mode" — Smart Campaigns hide the controls beginners most need to learn.
    3. Organize like this: Account → Campaigns (one per service) → Ad groups (one tight theme each) → Keywords + Ads. Example: campaign "AC Repair" → ad group "AC repair Dhaka" → ad group "AC servicing price Dhaka."

      4. Beginner rule: 5–15 closely related keywords per ad group. One messy ad group with 100 random keywords burns money.

      Keyword Match Types Explained Simply

      Match types control which searches trigger your ads — the single most important concept in Google Ads. Get it wrong and you pay for irrelevant clicks.

      • Broad match: Your ad can show for related searches — synonyms and loose variations. Keyword `ac repair` could trigger "ac installation," "refrigerator repair," even "ac technician jobs." Maximum reach, maximum waste risk.
      • Phrase match: Your ad shows when the search contains your keyword's meaning. Keyword `"ac repair dhaka"` triggers "best ac repair dhaka" and "ac repair dhaka price" — but not "ac installation dhaka" (different intent).
      • Exact match: Your ad shows for searches with the same meaning as your keyword — `[ac repair dhaka]` triggers "ac repair in dhaka," not "ac installation dhaka."

      Start with phrase and exact match. Avoid broad match until you are experienced — without careful negative keywords, it is the fastest way to waste a budget.

      Negative Keywords: Your Budget's Bodyguard

      Negative keywords tell Google which searches should never trigger your ads. This is where beginners save the most money.

      For an AC repair business, negate `jobs`, `free`, `diy`, `how to`, and `course` — blocking job seekers, freebie hunters, DIYers, and students.

      Weekly habit: check your Search Terms report for the actual phrases people typed, and add irrelevant ones as negatives — fifteen minutes saves thousands of taka a month.

      Writing Search Ads That Get Clicks

      Structure every ad like this:

      • Headline 1: the keyword ("AC Repair in Dhaka").
      • Headline 2: the main benefit ("Same-Day Service at Your Door").
      • Headline 3: trust or offer ("4.8★ Rated — Call Now").
      • Description: benefit + clear call to action ("Certified technicians. Upfront pricing. Book in 60 seconds.").

      Rules that work:

      • Match the searcher's words. Searched "emergency plumber"? Your headline should say "Emergency Plumber," not "Quality Plumbing Solutions."
      • Include numbers. Prices, ratings, years in business, response times — specifics beat vague claims.
      • One clear call to action. Call now, book online, get a quote — pick one per ad.
      • Use ad extensions. Add sitelinks, call extensions, and location extensions — they make your ad bigger and more visible, free.

      Write 2–3 ads per ad group. Keep the winner, replace the loser, repeat monthly.

      Setting Budgets and Bids

      Budget: Start with ৳300–1,000 per day per campaign. Clicks run ৳10–50 in many local niches; competitive ones (insurance, legal, study abroad) cost far more. Your daily budget should buy at least 10–20 clicks, or you will not get enough data.

      Bidding: Start with manual CPC or "Maximize clicks" with a max CPC cap. Google's automated bidding works well only after you have conversion tracking plus 2–4 weeks of data — automation with zero data is handing your wallet to a stranger.

      The math: a ৳30 click with a 1-in-20 conversion rate = ৳600 per customer. If that customer brings ৳5,000 profit, the ads print money. Always work backward from your numbers.

      Location Targeting in Bangladesh

      One of Google Ads' biggest strengths is showing ads only where your customers are:

      • Serve a city? Target Dhaka — or specific areas like Dhanmondi or Uttara with radius targeting.
      • Deliver nationwide? Target all of Bangladesh but exclude areas you cannot serve.
      • Critical setting: choose "Presence" targeting — NOT the default. The default shows your Dhaka-only ad to someone in London searching "Dhaka plumber," wasting your budget.
      • Check the geographic report monthly and exclude places you do not serve.

      Conversion Tracking Basics

      A conversion is the action you want: a call, a form signup, a WhatsApp click, a purchase. Without tracking, you know spend but not earnings.

      Minimum setup:

      1. Have a website? Install the Google tag and track form submissions and purchases (Google Tag Manager makes this manageable without coding).
      2. Phone-based business? Use call extensions with call reporting — Google counts calls from your ads as conversions.
      3. WhatsApp orders? Track WhatsApp button clicks as conversions. Imperfect, but it connects spend to inquiries.

        4. Rough tracking beats none. Businesses that track conversions improve monthly; those that do not keep guessing.

        Common Mistakes (And How to Avoid Them)

        1. Broad match with no negative keywords. The #1 budget killer. Start with phrase and exact match; build a negative keyword list from day one.
        2. Sending traffic to the homepage. An ad promising "AC repair in Dhaka" should land on a page about AC repair in Dhaka — not your generic homepage. Message match lifts conversions dramatically.
        3. No conversion tracking. Without it, you cannot tell winners from losers.
        4. Setting and forgetting. Check search terms weekly, bids biweekly, test ads monthly. Google Ads rewards active management.
        5. Ignoring mobile. Most Bangladeshi searchers are on phones. Your page must load fast with big tap-to-call buttons — no tiny forms.
        6. Generic keywords too early. "Shoes" is expensive and vague; "buy running shoes online Bangladesh" is cheaper and converts better.

          7. Realistic Expectations

          Be honest with yourself going in:

          • Weeks 1–2: Data collection. Clicks come in, conversions are thin — you are learning which keywords work.
          • Months 1–2: With weekly optimization, cost per conversion should fall noticeably.
          • Month 3+: A well-managed campaign becomes predictable — you know what ৳1,000/day buys in inquiries.

          Google Ads is not magic money — it amplifies what works. Solid offer, pricing, and follow-up? Ads scale them. Cannot close inquiries? Ads buy expensive lessons. Fix fundamentals first — then advertise.

          Offering Google Ads Management as a Service

          Google Ads managers are among the best-paid freelancers in digital marketing — and Imtiaz Khan points out that search-advertising skills are scarce among Bangladeshi freelancers, which means less competition for you. The path:

          Step 1: Get certified (free). Google Skillshop's free certifications teach the platform and give you a credential clients recognize.

          Step 2: Practice with real spend. Run campaigns for your own project or a local business — even ৳10,000 of managed spend teaches more than any course.

          Step 3: Specialize. "Google Ads for dental clinics" beats "I do Google Ads." Specialists charge more and find clients easier — pick a high-value industry like coaching, medical, or B2B.

          Step 4: Build a portfolio. Document before-and-after: cost per lead, conversion rate, search-term cleanup. Show real numbers.

          Step 5: Price for value. Beginners often charge ৳15,000–30,000/month locally or $300–800/month internationally, often plus 10–15% of ad spend as accounts grow. Our freelance pricing guide shows how to frame it.

          Pair Google Ads management with SEO: capture buyers today while building free traffic for tomorrow. Freelancers who offer both are hard to replace.

          Frequently Asked Questions

          How much does Google Ads cost in Bangladesh?

          You control the budget — start at ৳300/day. Clicks run ৳10–50 for local services; competitive niches like study abroad cost far more. You pay only when someone clicks.

          Is Google Ads better than Facebook ads?

          They do different jobs. Google captures people actively searching to buy; Facebook creates demand among people not searching yet. Service businesses usually start with Google; product brands often start with Facebook.

          Do I need a website to run Google Ads?

          You can send clicks to a Facebook page, a WhatsApp link, or use call-only ads. But even a one-page landing site converts far better and lets you track properly.

          What is a good click-through rate for Google Ads?

          For search ads, 3–5% CTR is decent; above 5% is strong. Below 2% means your ad does not match the search — rewrite headlines to mirror the keywords.

          How do I stop wasting money on irrelevant clicks?

          Three habits: (1) start with phrase and exact match; (2) review Search Terms weekly and add negatives; (3) set location targeting to "Presence" only.

          Can I manage Google Ads myself or should I hire an expert?

          Learn the basics yourself even if you plan to hire — you will brief and judge freelancers better.

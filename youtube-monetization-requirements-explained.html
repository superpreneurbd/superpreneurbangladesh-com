Every creator wants to know the same thing: when does YouTube start paying me? This guide explains exactly what YouTube requires, how the application works from Bangladesh, and the mistakes that get channels rejected.

Important: YouTube changes its policies from time to time. Treat the thresholds below as current at the time of writing and always verify on YouTube's official monetization page before making decisions.

The Two Paths: Full Monetization vs. Fan Funding

YouTube offers two levels of earning access:

YouTube Partner Program (full monetization)

This unlocks ad revenue - the main income source for most creators. To qualify, your channel needs:

  • 1,000 subscribers, AND
  • 4,000 valid public watch hours in the last 12 months, OR 10 million valid public Shorts views in the last 90 days

Both conditions must be met. The Shorts path exists because short-form creators accumulate views differently - but note that Shorts ad revenue per view is much lower than long-form.

Fan funding (lower threshold)

This unlocks channel memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, and shopping features:

  • 500 subscribers, AND
  • 3 public uploads in the last 90 days, AND
  • 3,000 valid public watch hours in the last 12 months, OR 3 million valid public Shorts views in the last 90 days

Fan funding alone does not include ad revenue. It is a stepping stone.

What "Valid" Means

YouTube only counts legitimate views:

  • Public videos only. Unlisted and private videos do not count.
  • Real human viewers. Views from bots, paid view services, or your own repeated refreshing do not count - and can get your channel terminated.
  • Within the time window. Watch hours older than 12 months roll off. You need 4,000 within the trailing 12 months, not lifetime.

The Application Process from Bangladesh

Once you hit the thresholds, apply inside YouTube Studio under the Earn tab. The process has three steps:

Step 1: Review the terms

Accept the YouTube Partner Program terms. Read them - they cover what content is and is not allowed to earn.

Step 2: Set up Google AdSense

You need an AdSense account to receive payments. If you do not have one, you will create it during the application.

  • Use your real legal name and address - this must match your payment documents.
  • YouTube will mail a PIN to your address for verification. In Bangladesh, this can take several weeks. Do not move or change the address mid-process.
  • AdSense has its own payment threshold (a minimum amount before they send money). Payments typically go out monthly once you cross it, via bank transfer or other available methods.

Step 3: Tax information (W-8BEN)

YouTube will ask for tax forms. As a Bangladeshi creator, you will typically complete a W-8BEN form declaring you are not a US taxpayer. This affects how much tax YouTube withholds from your US-sourced earnings. Fill it in carefully - wrong information delays everything.

Step 4: Channel review

YouTube reviews your channel manually. This usually takes a few days to a few weeks. They check whether your content follows the monetization policies - not just whether you hit the numbers.

Common Rejection Reasons (And How to Avoid Them)

Reused content. Channels that re-upload other people's videos, compile clips without commentary, or post slideshows of stock images get rejected. Your content must add original commentary, analysis, or educational value.

Repetitious content. Videos that are essentially the same thing repeated - template-based content with minor changes - get rejected even if you made them yourself.

Misleading metadata. Titles, thumbnails, or descriptions that promise something the video does not deliver.

Artificial traffic. Any use of view-buying, sub-for-sub schemes, or engagement pods. YouTube detects these patterns easily.

Policy violations. Copyright strikes, community guideline strikes, or content in restricted categories.

If rejected, you can reapply after 30 days. Fix the actual problem first - reapplying with the same content gets rejected again.

What Happens After Approval

Once accepted, you control which videos show ads and what ad formats appear. YouTube takes a percentage of ad revenue and pays you the rest through AdSense.

Reality check on earnings: ad revenue alone is modest for most channels. A channel with steady views in a business or finance niche earns meaningfully more per thousand views than an entertainment channel, because advertisers bid more for those audiences. But even then, most successful creators earn more from sources beyond ads - sponsorships, digital products, and services. See from YouTube to business: monetization beyond ads.

How to Reach the Thresholds Faster (Legitimately)

  • Make search-targeted videos. A tutorial that ranks in search brings watch hours every day for years. See YouTube SEO: rank your videos higher.
  • Publish consistently. Two videos a week for six months beats ten videos in one week followed by silence.
  • Improve click-through rate. More clicks on the same impressions means more watch hours. See thumbnail design: get more clicks.
  • Increase average view duration. Longer watch time per viewer means fewer total views needed. See how to write video scripts that retain viewers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Bangladeshi creators join the YouTube Partner Program?

Yes. Bangladesh is a supported country. You apply the same way as creators anywhere else, with AdSense handling payments.

How do I receive YouTube payments in Bangladesh?

Through Google AdSense, which pays via bank transfer once you cross the payment threshold. You will need a bank account that can receive the transfer - most major Bangladeshi banks work. See [Payoneer vs Wise for Bangladeshis](/payoneer-vs-wise-for-bangladeshis.html) for related payment options.

How long does the review take?

Typically a few days to a few weeks. Complex cases take longer. There is no way to speed it up - just make sure your channel is clean before applying.

Do Shorts views count toward the 4,000 watch hours?

No. Shorts views count toward the separate Shorts threshold (10 million in 90 days). Long-form watch hours and Shorts views are tracked on different paths.

What if my application is rejected?

You can reapply after 30 days. Read the rejection reason carefully, fix the underlying issue (usually reused or repetitious content), and only then reapply.

Is ad revenue enough to live on?

For most creators, no - especially in the beginning. Treat ad revenue as one stream among several.

Why hire from Bangladesh?

If you run a small agency, you know the problem: winning clients is hard enough without drowning in delivery. Hiring locally is expensive. Doing everything yourself does not scale. Bangladesh offers a third option that more agencies are discovering every year.

Strong English

A large share of educated Bangladeshis work comfortably in English — written and spoken. Briefs, feedback calls and client-ready copy are not a problem.

Timezone overlap

Bangladesh (UTC+6) overlaps the full UK and EU working day. Real-time collaboration, same-day turnarounds, no waiting overnight for replies.

Serious value

Senior-level skills at a fraction of Western hiring costs. Not "cheap labour" — skilled people whose cost of living lets them charge less while earning well.

Growing talent pool

Freelancing is mainstream in Bangladesh now. Designers, marketers, editors and developers with international client experience are easy to find — if you know where to look.

What Expert Asset Studio does

Expert Asset Studio is an execution partner for small agencies, founded by Imtiaz Khan. Instead of strategy decks and advice, the studio handles implementation — the actual work of getting digital marketing done, behind the scenes, while you keep the client relationship.

  • Paid advertising — Meta and Google ad campaigns, set up and managed.
  • Landing pages — pages built to convert, not just to look nice.
  • Video editing — marketing videos, shorts, ads.
  • Copywriting — ad copy, page copy, email sequences.
  • CRO — conversion rate optimisation: improving what already exists.
  • Automation — follow-up systems, email automation, workflow setup.

How it works

  1. You describe the work. Email imtiaz@superpreneur.bd with what you need delivered — a landing page, an ad campaign, a batch of video edits.

Frequently asked questions

Why hire freelancers from Bangladesh?
Three reasons: strong English communication, a timezone (UTC+6) that overlaps the full UK/EU working day, and excellent value — senior-level skills at rates far below Western hiring costs.
What services does Expert Asset Studio provide?
Behind-the-scenes execution for small agencies: paid advertising, landing pages, video editing, marketing copywriting, conversion rate optimization (CRO), and automation.
How does working with Expert Asset Studio work?
You describe what needs delivering — a landing page, an ad campaign, a video edit. The studio executes it behind the scenes while you keep the client relationship. Email imtiaz@superpreneur.bd to start the conversation.
Is the quality reliable compared to hiring locally?
Judge by work, not by location. Ask for samples of real work before committing, start with a small paid trial project, and scale up once the quality is proven. That process works the same wherever your hire sits.

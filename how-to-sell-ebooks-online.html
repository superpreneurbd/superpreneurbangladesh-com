How to Sell Ebooks Online: The Complete Guide for Beginners (2026)

IK
by Imtiaz Khan
Founder, Superpreneur Bangladesh
Updated November 2026
Free guides, no hype. We fund this site through our own ebooks, courses and services — never paid placements.

Selling ebooks online means you write a book once and get paid every time someone downloads it. There is no printing, no shipping and no stock to manage. You create the file, put it on a platform or your own sales page, and readers pay and download it in minutes. This guide shows you how the whole thing works — where to sell, how to price, how to get paid from Bangladesh, and how to get your first sales in 30 days. If you want the bigger picture first, start with our complete guide to the digital product business.

The short answer

  • What it is: you write a short, useful ebook once and sell the same file again and again. One file, unlimited buyers.
  • The 4 steps: pick a problem people already pay to solve, write a short guide that solves it, list it where buyers already are, and send readers to it every week.
  • What it costs: almost nothing to start. Writing tools are free, and most platforms only take a cut when you sell.
  • The Bangladesh catch: the hard part is not writing — it is getting paid. PayPal and Stripe do not work here, so you need a payment route that does. We cover that below.
  • Real numbers: our own first ebook made about ৳60,000 in about 2 months, sold beside a full-time job. That is a real result, not a promise — your result depends on your topic, your price and your readers.

How selling ebooks online works

An ebook business has four moving parts, and only four. If one of them is missing, sales stop — so it helps to see the whole chain before you start writing.

  1. Write. Pick one clear problem and solve it in 30–80 pages. Short and useful beats long and vague. Most first ebooks take 2–4 weeks of steady writing.
  2. Package. Turn the draft into a clean PDF or EPUB with a real cover. Readers judge a book by its cover in about 3 seconds, so this step matters more than most beginners think.
  3. List. Put the book where buyers can find it — a big marketplace, a simple sales page, or both. The listing needs a clear title, a short description and a price.
  4. Promote. Send readers to the listing every week: a YouTube video, a free chapter, an email list, a Facebook post. No traffic means no sales, however good the book is.

Notice what is not on the list: a publisher, an office, an ad budget, or permission from anyone. That is why ebooks are the usual first product for beginners — the whole chain fits in one person's evenings and weekends.

How to price your ebook

Price is where most beginners guess — and guessing wrong costs real sales. A price that is too low tells readers the book is not worth much. A price that is too high stops first-time buyers who do not know you yet. Use the bands that work in Bangladesh:

Price bandWhen to use itExample (labelled)
৳197–497Your first short ebook (30–60 pages) to a new audience100 copies × ৳497 = ৳49,700
৳497–1,000A complete guide with checklists, templates or video50 copies × ৳997 = ৳49,850
৳1,000+A deep system or course-style book with personal support20 copies × ৳1,497 = ৳29,940

These are the bands from our own pricing guides — a first ebook usually lands in the ৳200–1,000 range. Two rules make pricing easier. First, start low and raise the price after the first 20–30 buyers leave good feedback; a higher price with proof sells better than a high price with no proof. Second, never price by the page count. Readers pay for the result — how fast your book solves their problem — not for how many pages you wrote. Our full ebook pricing guide walks through testing a price step by step.

How to get your first 10 sales

Your first 10 sales almost never come from strangers finding your listing. They come from people who already know you a little — followers, friends, a small email list, a YouTube audience. The fastest path is a free chapter funnel:

  1. Give away the first chapter free. A reader who finishes a useful free chapter trusts you enough to pay for the rest. Put the free chapter behind a simple email signup, so every download builds your list.
  2. Make one video or post about the problem. Teach one idea from the book in public — a short YouTube video, a Facebook post, a LinkedIn note. End it with one line: the full step-by-step is in the ebook.
  3. Ask the first buyers for a review. Three honest reviews on your sales page are worth more than any ad. Ask in person, right after they finish reading.
  4. Repeat weekly. One useful post a week beats ten posts in one day and silence for a month. Sales from ebooks grow slowly, then suddenly — the list and the content stack up.

One last honest note: an ebook is a business, not a lottery ticket. The writers who earn from ebooks are the ones who keep writing, keep posting, and keep listening to readers. Your first book teaches you how to sell your second one — and the second one is usually where the real money starts.

Frequently asked questions

How much money can you make selling ebooks online?
It varies widely. Most beginners earn little in the first month — the book needs readers before it needs a price. Our own first ebook made about ৳60,000 in about 2 months, sold beside a full-time job. Treat that as one real example, not a promise: your result depends on your topic, your price, and how many readers you can reach every week.
Do I need a website to sell ebooks?
No. Marketplaces like Amazon KDP let you sell without any website, and many beginners in Bangladesh start by selling direct over Facebook or WhatsApp with bKash payment. A simple sales page of your own is worth adding later, because it keeps your full margin and your customer list.
How do I get paid for ebooks from Bangladesh?
Amazon KDP pays by bank transfer or Payoneer, which both work from Bangladesh. Gumroad and similar platforms need PayPal or Stripe, which are not available here. For Bangladeshi buyers, take bKash or Nagad directly and deliver the file by email or WhatsApp. Our guide to selling ebooks from Bangladesh without Stripe or PayPal explains every working route.
How long does it take to sell your first ebook?
Writing a short guide takes 2–4 weeks of steady work, and the first sale often comes in the first month if you already have readers — a YouTube audience, a Facebook following, or an email list. Without any audience yet, plan on 2–3 months: the writing is fast, building the readers takes longer.
